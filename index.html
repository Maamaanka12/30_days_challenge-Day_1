About Us

A legacy of healing

Shaafi Hospital has been at the forefront of healthcare in the region for over 15 years, combining medical excellence with genuine compassion.

Our mission is to make quality healthcare accessible to every family. From routine check-ups to complex surgeries, our multidisciplinary team works together to deliver personalized treatment plans that put patients first.

Equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic labs, modern operation theaters, and a dedicated emergency unit — we ensure every patient receives timely, accurate, and effective care.
